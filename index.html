How to play

A pair-matching game. The cards are shuffled face down and you have to remember where each one is.

  1. Pick a difficulty to set the size of the board.
  2. Click a card to flip it, then click a second card.
  3. Matching pairs stay face up.
  4. A mismatch flips back after a moment, so watch closely.
  5. Clear every pair to win. Moves, time and your best run are tracked.
